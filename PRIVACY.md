# Privacy Policy for Aloud

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Aloud reads text aloud with the Kokoro voice model. It downloads the voice model once from Hugging Face, then runs entirely on your Mac.

**Data collection.** Aloud does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to the developer. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

**Files you open.** Files stay on your device. Anything the app produces is written only where you choose to save it.

**Permissions.** Any system permission the app asks for (such as microphone, screen recording, camera, or Apple Health) is used only for the feature you turned on, and only while you use it.

**Contact.** Questions about this policy can be raised at https://github.com/Dunebru/aloud/issues.
